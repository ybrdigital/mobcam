1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how Yash Rayjada ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"), trading as YBR Digital, handles information in connection with your use of the MobCam - OBS Camera mobile application on Android and the companion MobCam OBS Studio Plugin on Windows.

We believe in radical data minimization. Our products are designed to deliver ultra-low latency, broadcast-quality video directly to your workstation without collecting, harvesting, or monetizing your personal data.

2. Zero Cloud Video/Audio Streaming Architecture

MobCam is strictly a local client-server pipeline:

  • Direct Local Transmission: Your phone hardware encodes camera video into H.264 video frames and microphone audio into AAC audio packets. These packets are transmitted directly across your local network (LAN) via TCP port 4747 to OBS Studio.
  • No Cloud Intermediaries: No video frames, photographs, or audio samples ever touch external servers, content delivery networks (CDNs), or third-party cloud storage.
  • USB Wired Mode: When connecting via USB (using Android ADB port forwarding), media data travels entirely through your physical USB cable with zero radio broadcast.

3. Android Application Permissions

To operate as a live webcam feed for OBS Studio, the Android application requests the following device permissions:

Permission Type Purpose & Functional Justification
android.permission.CAMERA Dangerous / Runtime Capturing the live camera feed, controlling hardware zoom, toggling device torch/flashlight, setting manual exposure compensation (EV), and configuring white balance.
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Dangerous / Runtime Capturing microphone audio (48kHz AAC) when phone audio streaming is enabled by the user. Audio is streamed in real time and is never recorded or saved to storage.
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Opening local TCP network sockets to deliver the video stream to OBS Studio on your PC, and communicating with Google Play Billing / RevenueCat for subscription validation.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE		 Normal Detecting local Wi-Fi connectivity, identifying the local IP address for socket binding, and listening for OBS Studio UDP discovery broadcasts.
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE		 Special / Service Ensuring the video and audio streaming service remains continuously active when the smartphone screen is locked, dimmed, or when navigating between apps during a broadcast.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (API 33+) Displaying the persistent foreground notification required by Android to inform the user that camera and microphone streaming is active.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK Normal Preventing the device CPU from entering sleep mode during active live broadcast sessions.

4. Information We Collect, Store & Process

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Smartphone

The app stores user configuration preferences locally in Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and includes:

  • Resolution preferences (e.g., 1080p, 4K, 720p).
  • Target framerate (30 FPS or 60 FPS).
  • Bitrate settings (8,000 kbps to 35,000 kbps).
  • Latency mode (Ultra-Low Latency vs. Balanced).
  • Audio transmission toggle (Enabled / Disabled).
  • Studio camera preferences (Torch status, Exposure value, White balance mode).

B. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • No User Accounts: No sign-up, registration, email address, username, or password required.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or calendar entries.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not access GPS, cellular location, or geographic coordinates.
  • No Media Files: We do not scan, access, read, or upload device photos, gallery albums, or personal files.
  • No Biometric or Facial Recognition: Camera pixels are encoded directly as raw YUV/RGB frames without facial recognition or biometric profiling.

5. Windows OBS Plugin & Installer Details

The companion MobCam OBS Studio Plugin runs as a native 64-bit DLL inside OBS Studio on Windows:

  • Local Web Control Server: The plugin hosts an embedded lightweight HTTP server strictly on http://localhost:4752/ (IPv4 loopback address 127.0.0.1). It is completely blocked from the outside internet and only serves the OBS Quick Controls dock on your local PC.
  • Zero Telemetry: The desktop plugin contains zero tracking scripts, analytics libraries, or outbound external internet connections.
  • Windows Installer: The setup installer (MobCam-OBS-Plugin-v1.0.0-Setup.exe) places plugin binaries in %ProgramData%\obs-studio\plugins\mobcam\ and automatically registers the browser dock in your local OBS configuration file (%APPDATA%\obs-studio\user.ini). It collects no personal information.

6. In-App Subscriptions & Financial Information

MobCam offers an optional paid upgrade called MobCam Pro, which unlocks watermark-free streaming, 4K UHD resolution, 60 FPS, ultra-low latency, and the Studio Camera Suite.

Payment Security Notice: All purchases and subscriptions are handled exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing. The developer does not receive, view, process, or store credit card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses.

To verify subscription status and maintain your Pro entitlement across app updates, we integrate RevenueCat Inc. (a subscription infrastructure platform). RevenueCat processes:

  • An anonymous, randomly generated App User ID.
  • Google Play order transaction receipts and product SKU identifiers.
  • Purchase timestamps and subscription expiration dates.

For more details, review the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

7. Diagnostic & Crash Reports

To diagnose and resolve application crashes across different Android devices, MobCam integrates Google Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google LLC).

When an unexpected error or crash occurs, anonymized diagnostic data is sent to Firebase, including:

  • Crash stack trace and code exception logs.
  • Device model name, manufacturer, and CPU architecture.
  • Android operating system version and free RAM/storage state.

This diagnostic information contains no camera video, audio recordings, or personal identifiers. For more details, consult the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because MobCam does not maintain external cloud databases of user accounts:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can completely erase all locally stored settings at any time by clearing the application's storage in your phone settings (Settings → Apps → MobCam → Storage → Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.
  • Subscription Cancellation: Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled at any time through your Google Play Store account (Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions).
