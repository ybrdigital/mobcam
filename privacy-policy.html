🔒 Our Core Privacy Guarantee

MobCam - OBS Camera does not record, upload, monitor, or transmit your video stream or microphone audio to any external cloud servers. All video and audio streams travel strictly peer-to-peer over your private local Wi-Fi network or direct USB cable to your personal computer running OBS Studio.

  • MobCam - OBS Camera (the Android mobile client application available on Google Play); and
  • MobCam OBS Studio Plugin (the Windows 64-bit plugin, installer, and integrated web dock running on your desktop PC).

We are committed to absolute transparency and data minimization. We treat your personal data with the highest standards of confidentiality and in strict compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), the Google Play Developer Policy, and global privacy standards.

2 Zero Cloud Streaming Architecture

Unlike cloud-dependent webcam solutions, MobCam is engineered entirely around a Local Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Architecture:

Direct Local Transmission
When you broadcast video and audio from your smartphone to OBS Studio:
  1. Video frames are compressed natively on your phone using hardware-accelerated H.264/AVC encoders.
  2. Microphone audio is compressed using low-overhead AAC encoding (48 kHz).
  3. The combined media stream is delivered across your private local area network (LAN) or direct USB cable via TCP port 4747 directly into your OBS Studio video pipeline.

No intermediate cloud servers exist. We do not operate relay servers, video storage servers, or media proxies. Neither the developer nor any third party has technical access to your camera frames, microphone audio, live broadcast feed, or recorded media.

3 Android Permissions & Usage

To function as an ultra-low latency professional streaming camera, the Android application requests specific runtime permissions. Each permission is utilized solely for its declared functional purpose:

Android Permission Type Justification & Usage Purpose
android.permission.CAMERA Dangerous / Runtime Required to capture the live camera sensor feed, provide digital/optical zoom, activate the device torch, adjust exposure compensation (EV), and manage manual white balance.
android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Dangerous / Runtime Required to capture live microphone audio (48kHz AAC) when phone audio streaming is enabled by the user. Audio is never stored on disk.
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Required to open local network sockets (TCP port 4747) to stream frames to OBS Studio on your local network, and communicate with Google Play Billing / RevenueCat for license verification.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE		 Normal Required to verify local Wi-Fi connectivity, discover the local IP address for socket binding, and listen for OBS Studio UDP discovery broadcasts.
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE		 Special / Service Allows the streaming pipeline to stay active uninterrupted when your phone screen turns off, dims, or when switching briefly to another application during live streaming.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (API 33+) Displays the required persistent foreground notification indicating that an active camera streaming session is running.
android.permission.WAKE_LOCK Normal Prevents the smartphone CPU from entering deep sleep during prolonged live broadcasts.

4 Data We Collect & Process

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

MobCam stores non-sensitive user preferences exclusively inside your phone's private Android SharedPreferences container. This includes:

  • Camera resolution preference (e.g., 1080p, 4K, 720p).
  • Target framerate (30 FPS or 60 FPS).
  • Video bitrate setting (e.g., 8,000 kbps to 35,000 kbps).
  • Latency mode selection (Ultra-Low Latency vs. Balanced).
  • Audio transmission toggle (On / Off).
  • Studio camera parameters (Torch state, Exposure value, White balance mode).

This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the application or clear app data.

B. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or create a password to use MobCam.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, home addresses, phone numbers, or social media handles.
  • No Location Data: We do not access GPS, cellular geolocation, or coarse location coordinates.
  • No Photos or Files: MobCam has zero access to your device's photo gallery, internal storage, or personal files.
  • No Biometric or Facial Recognition: Camera frames are decoded as raw color pixels without facial recognition, facial tracking, or biometric profiling.

5 Windows OBS Plugin & Web Dock

The MobCam OBS Studio Plugin (Windows 64-bit) runs locally as a native DLL module inside OBS Studio. Its privacy parameters are as follows:

  • Local Loopback Web Server: The plugin launches an embedded lightweight HTTP server listening strictly on http://localhost:4752/ (IPv4 loopback 127.0.0.1). This server is inaccessible from external networks or the internet; it only serves the OBS Quick Controls dock on your local machine.
  • Zero Outbound Telemetry: The desktop plugin contains no telemetry, user tracking, phone-home mechanisms, or automated usage analytics.
  • Inno Setup Windows Installer: The installer executable (MobCam-OBS-Plugin-v1.0.0-Setup.exe) checks for OBS Studio installation paths, stops running instances of OBS with user consent to prevent file-lock errors, installs files into %ProgramData%\obs-studio\plugins\mobcam\, and registers the browser dock in your user profile (%APPDATA%\obs-studio\user.ini). It collects zero personal telemetry.

6 In-App Subscriptions & Payment Processing

MobCam offers an optional auto-renewing subscription and in-app purchase named MobCam Pro, unlocking watermark-free video, 4K streaming, 60 FPS, ultra-low latency, and the Studio Camera Suite.

Zero Access to Financial Credentials
All financial transactions, credit card handling, bank processing, tax collection, and billing management are executed strictly and exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing. The developer does not process, receive, or store credit card numbers, PayPal details, or billing addresses.

To maintain active license entitlements across app updates and reinstalls, we integrate RevenueCat Inc. (a leading in-app subscription infrastructure provider). RevenueCat processes:

  • An anonymous, randomly generated App User ID.
  • Google Play transaction identifier and product ID.
  • Purchase timestamp and subscription expiration date.

For more information on RevenueCat's data protection standards, please consult the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

7 Diagnostics & Crash Reports

To ensure reliable broadcast stability and resolve critical video decoding bugs across thousands of Android device configurations, MobCam uses Google Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google LLC).

When an unhandled application crash occurs, Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects anonymized diagnostic logs:

  • Stack trace and code exception details.
  • Smartphone brand, device model, and CPU architecture.
  • Android OS version and security patch level.
  • RAM usage and internal storage state at the time of failure.

These diagnostics contain zero video frames, audio samples, or personal identity markers, and are used solely to debug stability issues. For details, refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

8 Data Retention & Security

Because MobCam operates on a zero-cloud, client-side model:

  • No Server Retention: We store no personal databases, no stream archives, and no user profiles on any external servers.
  • Device Security: Communication between your smartphone and PC occurs over local TCP sockets. When connected via USB cable (ADB port forwarding), data packets never traverse wireless airwaves.
  • Instant Deletion: You can completely erase all locally stored preferences at any moment by navigating to Settings → Apps → MobCam → Clear Data on your Android device, or by uninstalling the app.
