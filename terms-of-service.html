By using MobCam, you understand and acknowledge the technical demands of high-definition video processing:

  • Device Thermal Output: Continuously streaming high-bitrate video (especially 1080p60 or 4K60) engages high-power camera sensors and on-chip hardware video encoders, generating substantial heat. You are responsible for ensuring adequate device ventilation.
  • Battery Consumption: Camera hardware encoding draws significant electrical power. Connecting your smartphone to a suitable charger or USB 3.0 power port during prolonged streaming sessions is strongly recommended.
  • Local Network Dependency: Wireless streaming performance (bitrate stability, dropped frames, packet jitter) is fundamentally dependent on your local Wi-Fi router quality, channel congestion, distance, and 5 GHz frequency band availability. The developer cannot guarantee zero packet drop across degraded or congested Wi-Fi networks.
